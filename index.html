The 6 Pillars of Transformation

Swaleh's Manifesto 2027

A comprehensive, measurable blueprint for Ang'urai East Ward, Teso North.

🌾 Agricultural Transformation & Value Addition

Immediate Priority — Subsidized high-yield maize, soya, and cassava cuttings. Cassava & Grain Milling Mini-Plant. Rehabilitation of 4 cattle dips (Moding, Kakurikit, Aboloi, Chelelemuk). Free bi-annual poultry & dairy vaccination drives. Ward Agriculture Equipment Hire Service with tractors at 60% below commercial rates.

🛣️ All-Weather Roads & Solar Clean Water

Immediate Priority — Gravel and maintain key feeder roads, install solar streetlights in markets, improve drainage, and ensure clean borehole water for every village.

📚 100% Transparent Bursaries & ECDE Excellence

High Priority — Increase bursary allocation with full transparency, build a digital hub, partner with TVETs for youth skills training, and improve ECDE facilities.

🏥 Equipped Dispensaries & Maternal Health

High Priority — Equip Aloete and Changara health centers with maternity wings, ambulances, and essential medicines. Promote maternal and child health.

👩‍💼 Youth, Bodaboda & Women Enterprise Fund

Strategic Priority — Empower women and youth groups, support bodaboda riders with financing, and strengthen community peace committees. 30% of Ward Economic Stimulus focused here.

🤝 Accountable Representation & Citizen Barazas

Strategic Priority — Foster coexistence, dialogue, and community cohesion through regular barazas. Every shilling accounted for down to the last cent.

    • Who is Swaleh?

    A leader rooted in community

    Swaleh Chemasuet is a community mobilizer, youth leader, and a proud ODM member. He has spent years working with farmers, women groups, and youth forums in Ang'urai East. His vision is to bridge the gap between the people and the county government, ensuring that resources are used transparently and effectively.

    His manifesto, "Edeke Eremit — Maendeleo kwa Wote", is built on 6 pillars of transformation covering agriculture, infrastructure, education, healthcare, youth/women empowerment, and accountable governance.

    "I give my word to the mothers of Moding, the farmers of Kakurikit, the youth of Ang'urai, and the elders of Kolanya: every shilling meant for this pillar will be accounted for down to the last cent."
